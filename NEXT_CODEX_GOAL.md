snapshot from the current `daily-nas-broad/v1` cache, without opening any KIS,
credential, broker, model, or training route.

## Authority And Boundaries

- This is an offline Data and temporary Validation package. It must not read
  `.env`, construct a KIS client, make a network call, or touch account,
  position, quote, order, live, model, GPU, or dashboard code.
- Read the collector index but never take its worker lock, edit its cache, or
  block its scheduled continuation. A changing index is a scoped retry, not a
  company hold.
- Keep all outputs under `D:\market_data` or
  `D:\thericher-v2\model-artifacts`; do not place generated bytes in Git.
- Preserve limitations visibly. A successful comparison proves only retained
  overlap immutability for the two snapshots, not provider correctness,
  adjustment equivalence, session finality, historical membership, or a
  training-ready universe.
