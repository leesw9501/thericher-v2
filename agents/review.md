# Review Stateboard (Retired)

## Status

Retired as a durable lane on 2026-07-18. Review is an invoked integration
checkpoint; Claude is a falsification-first challenger at the bias-prone
boundaries defined in `AGENTS.md`.

## Current Review

At integration, check only whether the change added unnecessary:

- documents or report families,
- gates or promotion packets,
- artifact-specific helpers or job kinds,
- workers, schedulers, coordinators, or dashboards,
- duplicated agent memory or status history,
- constraints that delay KIS paper without reducing execution risk.

## Claude Boundary

Claude challenges leakage/survivorship changes, breadth-to-depth selection,
sealed holdout access, unexpectedly strong results, ensembles, KIS paper/live
authority or material risk changes, incident recovery, and major architecture
or worker growth. It does not review routine implementation or tests.

Claude is advisory. Emergency containment never waits for review. `KIS_PAPER_*`
standing authority is defined in `AGENTS.md`; the operator retains live
credential/live-capital, paid, rights, public-exposure, and business authority.
Codex integrates technical resolutions.

## Recovery

Review owns no runtime resource. A review is recoverable from the named claim,
frozen evidence IDs, Claude response when used, Codex resolution, and linked
Git/artifact evidence. Do not create recurring review reports.
